# Scope and claims

GhostFace is currently a research-stage photo privacy utility. Implemented functions are local image validation, orientation, metadata removal and visual fidelity comparison. These do not prevent copying or facial identity cloning.

Do not describe metadata removal, fidelity metrics, or six provisional settings as AI protection. Do not publish effectiveness percentages or imply immunity. A practical alternative for public sharing is to reduce public exposure, remove location metadata and use visible redaction when anonymity is needed. Provenance or watermarking can signal ownership but does not prevent copying.

A public repository contains no private photos or third-party model weights. User-facing website and deployment are not implemented.
