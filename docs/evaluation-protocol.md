# Evaluation protocol (draft)
Register method versions, rights, provenance, consent and hardware before any study. Separate development and held-out data. Record image fidelity, human review, cold and warm runtime, failure rates and independently reviewed category-specific evidence. Record all failures and uncertainty. Provisional conservative fidelity targets: SSIM >= 0.99 and PSNR >= 40 dB; these do not prove protection. No effectiveness threshold until baseline evidence and protocol review. No personal images uploaded or committed. Release requires licensing, privacy and security gates.
