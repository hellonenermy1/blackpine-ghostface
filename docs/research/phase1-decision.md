# Research decision — original objective retained
Owner instruction: retain the original anti-cloning research objective. Do not substitute a metadata utility as fulfillment of that objective.

Status: research-only, no validated production engine. The prior 35 requirements remain preserved and no acceptance criteria are waived. The six proposed settings are not validated and must not appear as effective protection options.

A peer-reviewed publication is evidence of what its authors report, not evidence that GhostFace reproduces it. Further work on literature synthesis, independent review, ethical governance and non-identifying image fidelity can proceed. No operational instructions or implementation for evading facial recognition or identification are included in this review.

Scientific acceptance remains blocked. Engineering tests are not scientific validation. No deployment, no personal data collection, no model redistribution. A qualified independent research team would need to establish efficacy, rights and browser feasibility before any production claim.
